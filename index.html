Japan is an island country in East Asia. It has four main islands: Hokkaido, Honshu, Shikoku, and Kyushu.

Most of Japan is covered by mountains. Mount Fuji is the tallest mountain and is important to Japanese culture.

Japan has four seasons. Spring has cherry blossoms, summer is warm, autumn has colorful leaves, and winter can be cold.

Tokyo is the capital city. Other famous cities include Kyoto, Osaka, Hiroshima, and Yokohama.

Japan is known for robots, bullet trains, electronics, and advanced technology.
