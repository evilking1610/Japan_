Tokyo

Tokyo is packed with fun, from crossing Shibuya’s famous scramble and shopping in Harajuku to exploring anime culture in Akihabara. Visitors can enjoy theme parks like Disneyland and DisneySea, futuristic Odaiba with its giant Gundam, or neon-filled Shinjuku nightlife. With unique cafés, karaoke, street food, and digital art at TeamLab, Tokyo offers nonstop excitement.

Kyoto

Kyoto – Kyoto is full of old temples, bamboo forests, and cool shrines. You can walk through the red gates at Fushimi Inari, see the shiny Golden Temple, and wear a kimono in the old streets of Gion. Don’t miss the cute monkeys at Arashiyama too!

Osaka

Osaka is a city that loves food and fun. Try yummy snacks like takoyaki (octopus balls) and okonomiyaki (pancakes). Walk around Dotonbori to see bright lights everywhere, and visit Universal Studios Japan for awesome rides.
